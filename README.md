# FROZEN SIGNAL

A 2D top-down survival horror game set in an abandoned Soviet research laboratory beneath the Antarctic ice.

Inspired by **SIGNALIS** - featuring detailed pixel art, a cold color palette, retrofuturistic aesthetics, and psychological horror.

## Story

You awaken in Outpost Erebus, a multi-level research facility that has been silent for decades. The base is partially frozen, with frost creeping across walls, frozen corpses mid-action, and machinery that stutters to life unpredictably.

*They weren't drilling for oil. They were listening. And something answered.*

## Features

- **Survival Horror Gameplay**: Limited inventory, scarce resources, and deadly enemies
- **Sanity/Cold System**: Stay too long in unheated areas and vision distorts; sanity drains in darkness
- **Atmospheric Horror**: CRT effects, scanlines, limited color palette, and oppressive atmosphere
- **Multiple Endings**: Your discoveries and choices determine your fate
- **Environmental Storytelling**: Scattered notes, audio logs, and fragmented memories reveal the truth
- **Glitchy Enemies**: Researchers twisted by something found deep in the ice, moving in uncanny patterns

## Installation

Requires Python 3.8+ and Pygame.

```bash
# Install dependencies
pip install -r requirements.txt

# Run the game
python main.py
```

## Game Systems

### Survival Mechanics
- **Health**: Damaged by enemies and extreme cold
- **Sanity**: Drains in darkness and near enemies; low sanity causes visual distortion
- **Warmth**: Depletes in unheated areas; find heaters or use heat packs

### Inventory
- Limited to 6 slots - choose your items wisely
- Keycards, weapons, healing items, documents, and puzzle items
- Some items can stack

### Combat
- Melee attacks with found weapons
- Firearms available but ammunition is scarce
- Enemies can be avoided or confronted

## The Facility

Outpost Erebus contains multiple areas to explore:

- **Crew Quarters** - Where you awaken
- **Main Corridor** - Frozen and dangerous
- **Research Laboratory** - What were they studying?
- **Storage Area** - Supplies and secrets
- **Reactor Control** - The heart of the base
- **Communications** - Can anyone hear you?
- **Deep Excavation** - What lies beneath?

## Endings

Your fate depends on what you discover and the choices you make. There are multiple possible endings:

- The Embrace
- The Silence
- The Escape
- The Truth
- Signal Lost
